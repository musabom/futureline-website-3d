🌍 6-Day Oman Discovery – Culture, Coast, Desert & Mountains

Duration: 6 Days / 5 Nights
Style: Private Guided Tour
Category: Culture • Nature • Heritage • Adventure

🗓 Day 1 – Muscat City Tour
A Journey Through Culture and Majesty

Duration: Approx. 6–7 Hours
Number of Stops: 7 Main Stops

1️⃣ Sultan Qaboos Grand Mosque

Begin your journey at one of the most magnificent mosques in the Islamic world. Admire the grand Swarovski crystal chandelier and the world’s second-largest handwoven Persian carpet.

🕒 Duration: 45 minutes
🎟 Admission: Free

2️⃣ Royal Opera House Muscat

Explore a masterpiece of architecture blending modern luxury with traditional Omani craftsmanship. A symbol of Oman’s cultural refinement and global vision.

🕒 Duration: 45 minutes
🎟 Admission: Not included – Approx. 2 OMR per person

3️⃣ Mutrah Fish Market

Experience authentic coastal life as local fishermen sell their daily catch in this vibrant traditional market.

🕒 Duration: 15 minutes
🎟 Admission: Free

4️⃣ Mutrah Corniche

Enjoy a scenic waterfront walk with views of traditional dhows, the harbor, and historic watchtowers.

🕒 Duration: 15 minutes
🎟 Admission: Free

5️⃣ Mutrah Souq

Step into one of Oman’s oldest marketplaces. Discover spices, frankincense, silver jewelry, traditional garments, and souvenirs.

🕒 Duration: 45 minutes
🎟 Admission: Free

6️⃣ Al Alam Palace & Jalali & Mirani Forts

Visit the ceremonial palace of His Majesty the Sultan, flanked by the iconic 16th-century Portuguese forts. Entry is not permitted, but the exterior provides spectacular photo opportunities.

🕒 Duration: 30 minutes
🎟 Admission: Exterior view only

🏛 Optional Museum Visits (Based on Time & Interest)
A) Bait Al Zubair Museum

Private museum showcasing traditional Omani heritage and daily life.
🕒 30–45 minutes
🎟 Approx. 3 OMR per person

B) National Museum of Oman

Modern museum presenting Oman’s history and maritime legacy.
🕒 1 hour
🎟 Approx. 5 OMR per person

C) Ghalya’s Museum of Modern Art

A charming museum bridging traditional and modern Omani life.
🕒 30 minutes
🎟 Approx. 2 OMR per person

🗓 Day 2 – Wadi Shab, Bimmah Sinkhole & Sur Coastal Tour

Duration: 8–9 Hours
Main Stops: 3 + Sur Highlights

1️⃣ Bimmah Sinkhole

A stunning natural limestone crater filled with turquoise water, perfect for swimming or photos.

🕒 45 minutes
🎟 Free

2️⃣ Wadi Shab

One of Oman’s most famous natural attractions. After a short boat crossing (approx. 1 OMR), hike through palm-lined canyons and swim through clear pools to reach a hidden waterfall cave.

🕒 4 hours (hike + swim + rest)
🎟 Free
🛶 Boat Fee: Approx. 1 OMR

3️⃣ Sur Coastal City Highlights
A) Sur Dhow Shipyard

Witness handcrafted wooden dhows in one of the region’s last traditional shipyards.
🕒 30 minutes
🎟 2 OMR

B) Al Ayjah Watchtower & Lighthouse

Panoramic harbor views from this historic defense point.
🕒 15 minutes
🎟 Free

C) Sur Corniche & Local Market

Relax along the waterfront and explore everyday life in Sur.
🕒 30–45 minutes
🎟 Free
